# Math & Stats BBQ
The Annual BBQ is our primary recruitment event, attracting new members and generating interest in mathematics. This all-day event features membership sign-ups, book sales, and interactive discussions about the field, all while enjoying a BBQ. The event is a cornerstone of our outreach efforts, helping us to build a strong and engaged community of math enthusiasts. By fostering a sense of belonging and excitement, this event supports our goal of expanding our membership and increasing participation in our activities.
